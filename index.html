About

Dany Entezari is a mathematician and programmer specializing in mathematical statistics and information retrieval. He has consulted and trained professionals in engineering and mathematics at Fortune 500 companies, universities, and government agencies—including IBM, AWS, Google, Halliburton, Cleveland Clinic, and Standard Chartered. He is also the founder of Bignumber, a computational research company focused on information retrieval and applications in the quantitative sciences. MSc in High Performance Computing from the University of Edinburgh.
